## Organisms / products

### `ProductsCardGrid`

H3 heading over a grid of grey (#eff1f4) 20px-rounded product cards from investia.ca's Products and services page. Each card has an H4 title, optional bold subtitle, one or more paragraphs and an outlined pill link pinned to the card bottom so buttons align across a row (hover turns it #2962ff; external links open in a new tab with the external icon). layout="thirds" gives three columns from 768px; "two-thirds" makes each card two columns wide (the insurance card). Cards stack full width on phones with full-width buttons.

## Templates

### `SiteShell`

Shared Investia page chrome for every route: site nav, <main id="main-content">, footer and CIRO/OCRI regulatory strip, in EN or FR. Nav, footer and regulatory copy come from the homepage EN/FR dictionary. The `page` key (src/content/site/routes.ts) points the language toggle at the same page in the other language and the logo at that locale's homepage, so the toggle never leaves the site, and marks the matching menu link as the current page. Pass page sections as children.

type: component · tags: layout, navigation, footer, bilingual · source: `src/components/templates/site-shell.tsx` · [Storybook](https://xnsengo.github.io/poly-storybook-demp/?path=/docs/templates-siteshell--docs)
